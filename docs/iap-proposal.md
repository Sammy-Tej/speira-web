# Speira: in-app purchase proposal

A draft for Sam to read and react to, 16/10/26. Nothing is decided yet. The open questions are at the end.

## Sam's decisions (17/10/26)
- **Packs plus Everything, all one-time purchases.** Studio Tools is split in two, which makes six packs:
  - More Pieces;
  - Fancy Pens;
  - Shape Studio;
  - **Layout Tools** (rulers, guides, grid, snapping, canvas sheet, Repeat, two sets);
  - **Export Pro** (SVG for plotters, video up to 4K, CMYK later);
  - Unwind+.
- **Prices:** around **£1.99** per pack, with £1.99–£4.99 acceptable. Everything is priced so it's clearly the best deal.
- **Try before you buy for every pack**: try it on the paper, decide when you save or export.
- **1.0 ships with two packs on sale: More Pieces and Fancy Pens.** The other packs arrive in **1.1**, along with the tip jar.
- **The Apple TV app is free and includes the core Unwind.** Just watch is now called Unwind (Sam, 17/10/26).

## 1. What we're aiming for

Speira is for four kinds of people at once:
- **Beginners** trying illustration for the first time.
- **Scribblers** passing the time.
- **Background watchers** who leave Unwind running.
- **Geeks** who want every number and export option.

The money side has to suit all four:

1. **The free app is complete, not a demo.** Your 1.0 list is a whole kit: rings, wheels, pens, paper, layers, Sketchbook, PNG and JPG export, and Unwind. Someone who never pays should still love it.
2. **Pay once, own it forever.** No subscriptions. Speira works offline and costs you nothing per user to run (iCloud is Apple's), so a subscription would feel like rent for nothing new.
3. **No ads, ever, and no tracking.** This keeps kids' mode simple and keeps the App Store privacy label clean ("Data Not Collected").
4. **Never interrupt drawing or watching.** The shop is something you visit. It never pops up in the middle of a pattern, and never appears in Unwind or on Apple TV while it plays.
5. **Buy once, use everywhere.** A purchase on iPad unlocks the same thing on Mac and Apple TV (Apple's "Universal Purchase"), and it's shared with family (Family Sharing).

## 2. The models we could choose

| Model | How it works | Good | Bad | Fit for Speira |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Paid up front** | £3–£10 to download, everything included | Simple; no shop to design | Most people never try paid apps; one price can't suit both scribblers and geeks; competitors start at free | Poor. It fights your "for everyone" aim |
| **B. Free + one-time packs** (recommended) | Free core; optional themed packs; an "Everything" bundle | Each audience buys only what it wants; packs grow with new features; low first price (£1.99) | More products to manage; packs need clear names | **Best fit.** It matches progressive disclosure: the depth is there when you want it |
| **C. Free + one "Pro" unlock** | One purchase unlocks all extras | Simplest shop | Scribblers who only want glitter pens pay for geek tools; one big price stops impulse buys | Good as a *second* option inside B (the Everything bundle) |
| **D. Subscription** | Monthly or yearly | Steady income | Disliked for offline creative tools; needs constant new content; Apple scrutinises it more | Poor. Against principle 2 |
| **E. Free + tip jar** | Everything free; optional "thank you" tips | Very friendly | Low, unpredictable income | Good as an extra later, not the main model |

**Market check** (from the IP research): the most-rated competitor, Spirograph Maker, is free with ads plus an ad-removal purchase. SpiroDesigner is £2.99 up front, and Spirograph Drawing is £9.99 up front. A free, ad-free app with one-time packs between £1.99 and £9.99 sits in the same range, and is friendlier than any of them.

## 3. Recommended packs

Every pack is a **non-consumable** in-app purchase: bought once, restorable on every device, shareable with family.

| Pack | What it unlocks | Who it's for | Price (UK / US) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **More Pieces** | Bars (84, 108), oval, triangle and square rings (with their inner edges), fixed wheel (wheel on wheel), and every future piece | Everyone who wants new shapes | £2.99 / $2.99 |
| **Fancy Pens** | Metallic and glitter, plus future special pens (neon, chalk, ink wash…) | Scribblers, beginners | £1.99 / $1.99 |
| **Shape Studio** | Make your own shapes, the My shapes folder, inner edges for your shapes | Makers, kids with grown-ups, geeks | £3.99 / $3.99 |
| **Studio Tools** | New canvas sheet (any size, DPI, colour profiles, later CMYK), SVG export (with pen effects or plain lines for plotters and cutters), video export up to 4K, rulers, guides, grid and snapping, recipes, Repeat, two sets at once | Geeks, designers, printers, plotter owners | £4.99 / $4.99 |
| **Unwind+** | Unwind extras: two at once, all colour moods (including Kaleidoscope, Jewels, Earth, My pens), "Use my drawing", layers on screen, session codes for sharing. On Apple TV: the same | Background watchers | £2.99 / $2.99 |
| **Everything** | All five packs, now and future, at a discount | Fans, and anyone who hates choosing | £9.99 / $9.99 (separately: £16.95) |

**Notes:**
- **Future features join a pack, never a new paywall.** If you own Fancy Pens and we add a chalk pen, you get it free. That builds trust and makes "Everything" feel worth it.
- **Splitting Studio Tools.** It is deliberately broad. If you'd rather sell "Export Pro" (SVG, video, 4K) separately from "Layout Tools" (rulers, guides, snapping, canvas sheet), that works too. Price both at £2.99 and keep Everything at £9.99.
- **Prices are examples.** Apple has fixed price points, and UK prices include VAT. Before deciding, we should look at what similar one-time creative apps charge. The research couldn't verify benchmarks beyond the three direct competitors.

## 4. What stays free forever

These are Sam's 1.0 list:
- **Pieces:** both round rings and all 18 wheels; pen holes, inside and outside, moving the ring.
- **Drawing:** by hand with momentum, by itself, finish, lift the pen, clicks, preview, undo, Surprise me.
- **Pens:** ballpoint, gel, fineliner, felt-tip, highlighter and pencil; any colour, rainbow ink, thickness, opacity.
- **Paper:** portrait/landscape, paper colours and textures; one sheet shaped like your iPad's screen.
- **Layers:** hide, delete, reorder; clear paper.
- **View:** zoom, pan, turn, full screen, hide panels.
- **Files:** all of the Sketchbook and files.
- **Export:** PNG (with see-through) and JPG.
- **Unwind:** the core (it draws forever, black paper, Auto mood, bedtime fade).
- **Comfort:** Help, light/dark, piece colours, keyboard shortcuts, touch and Pencil comfort.

**Rule of thumb:** anything that makes the *first ten minutes* good is free. Anything you go looking for once you're hooked is a pack.

## 5. Try before you buy

People buy what they've already enjoyed, so locked features should be **visible and playable**:

- **Try on the paper.** Locked pieces and pens can be picked and used on the canvas. A small padlock badge sits on them.
- **The moment it matters.** Only when you **save or export** a drawing that uses a locked item does a calm sheet appear: "This drawing uses Fancy Pens. Unlock them for £1.99, or keep drawing and decide later." Choosing "later" keeps the drawing, which can still be saved, without the locked layers in its export.
  - *Alternative (simpler, also common):* exports of locked items get a small "Speira" watermark until unlocked. I prefer the first option; watermarks feel cheap.
- **Unwind+:** extras show in the Unwind menu with a padlock. Tapping one plays it for 60 seconds, then offers the unlock.
- **Studio Tools:** rulers and guides work fully while visible; the unlock is asked for when you **export** SVG or video, or **save** a custom canvas.

## 6. The shop

- **One place:** a **Speira Shop** sheet, reached from Settings, from a padlock badge, or from the Sketchbook's menu.
- **Each pack** shows a short animated example drawn with it, what's inside, the price, and **"Included in Everything"**.
- **Always present:**
  - **Restore Purchases** (Apple requires it).
  - **Family Sharing:** a note that purchases are shared.
  - "Already bought on another device? Restore."
- **Never:**
  - Countdown timers or fake discounts.
  - Prompts during drawing or watching.
  - Asking more than once per session.
  - A shop on Apple TV while Watch plays.
- **Thank-you moment:** after buying, a short celebration and the new pieces or pens "dropping into" the dock.

---

## 7. Kids' mode

- Kids' mode hides the shop completely. Any purchase, or link outside the app, sits behind a **grown-up check**, such as "hold two fingers for 3 seconds and type the year you were born" (Apple's Guideline 1.3).
- Stay a normal-audience app with a kids' *mode*, not in the Kids Category. The category is a one-way commitment with stricter rules.
- A pack bought by a grown-up works in kids' mode too.

---

## 8. Mac and Apple TV (version 2.0)

- **Universal Purchase:** one App Store record covers iPad, Mac and Apple TV, so packs bought on one work on all of them. This is set up when we create the App Store record, so it has to be decided **before 1.0 launches**.
- **Apple TV:**
  - The shop exists but is small: the Unwind+ unlock and "Restore".
  - The free Apple TV app shows the core Unwind.
  - Unwind+ unlocks the extras there too.
- **Mac:** identical packs. Studio Tools shines most on Mac (rulers, guides, SVG for plotters).

## 9. Launch plan

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **1.0 launch** | Free core plus **two packs ready**: **More Pieces** and **Fancy Pens**. Both are already built and proven on the web, so they bring income from day one without delaying launch. **Everything** is also on sale ("all future packs included") for early fans. |
| **1.1 / 1.2** | **Studio Tools** and **Unwind+** arrive as their features land on iPad. People who bought Everything get them automatically. |
| **2.0** | **Shape Studio** (with make your own shapes), Mac and Apple TV. Universal Purchase means existing buyers have them on every device on day one. |
| **Later** | An optional **tip jar** ("Buy Sam a coffee") for people who want to give more. Seasonal free **colour moods** to keep people coming back. |

**Launch idea:** an **"Early supporter" price for Everything** (for example £6.99 for the first month), clearly dated. It rewards the first fans without a fake countdown.

## 10. What the paid Developer Program has to do with this

In-app purchases can only be built and **tested** (with Apple's test "sandbox" accounts and StoreKit testing) once you've joined the paid Developer Program. Products also have to be created in App Store Connect and sent for review alongside the app, which takes days.

So if 1.0 launches with packs, you'll need to join **a few weeks before going public**, not on the day. See the main reply for the recommended timing.

---

## 11. Rough maths (an illustration only, not a forecast)

Free creative apps with one-time unlocks often see a **small percentage** of users buy something.

Assume, for illustration only, **3% buy one £2.99 pack and 1% buy Everything at £9.99**. Then every **1,000 downloads** bring about **£190 before Apple's cut**, or about **£160 after Apple's 15%** (Small Business Program).

The real figures will differ. The point is that **Everything** carries a lot of the income, so it should be prominent and feel generous.
